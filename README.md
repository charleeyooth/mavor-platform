# MAVOR Platform

### Business Operations Platform for MAVOR Cleaning Services

MAVOR is a business operations platform designed to support the day-to-day workflows of a professional cleaning company across customer, employee, and administrative experiences.

This repository is a **public portfolio showcase**. The production source code, credentials, private business logic, and operational data are not included.

## Product Overview

The platform brings together booking, scheduling, customer access, employee workflows, administration, payments, and service operations in one connected system.

## Core Experiences

- Client portal
- Employee portal
- Admin portal
- Quick Service booking flow
- Service-window capacity management
- Booking confirmation workflows
- Employee clock-in / clock-out
- Emergency visit closure
- Payment and refund workflows
- PWA experience
- Virtual assistant for service guidance
- Email and customer-support workflows

## Quick Service

Customers can choose from service windows such as Morning, Afternoon, Late Afternoon, and Anytime. Capacity is managed per window so unavailable periods can be blocked without disrupting existing reservations.
